# Licensing, rights, and responsible production

## Software

Before using or redistributing software, verify the exact repository license, version/commit, dependencies, and attribution obligations. A repository's top-level license may not cover every bundled asset or dependency.

## Media

Use only media the user owns, has permission to use, or has licensed for the intended purpose. Check commercial use, derivative works, attribution, territory, duration, and platform restrictions.

## People, voices, and likeness

Obtain informed consent before cloning or materially simulating a real person's face or voice. Do not create deceptive endorsements or imply a person said something they did not say. Follow applicable platform disclosure rules.

## Music and sound

Use original, public-domain, or properly licensed music and sound effects. Keep evidence of the license and verify that social, advertising, and commercial uses are covered.

## AI disclosure

Where required or appropriate, disclose synthetic or materially altered media. Do not present fictional reenactments as documentary footage or fabricated facts as verified reporting.
