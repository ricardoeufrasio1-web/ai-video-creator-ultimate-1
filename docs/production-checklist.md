# Production checklist

Use this checklist for any niche, platform, language, or video format. Mark a stage complete only when its evidence exists.

## Brief and strategy
- [ ] Audience, objective, platform, aspect ratio, duration, language, tone, and CTA are defined.
- [ ] Required facts, claims, source material, and exact wording are identified.
- [ ] Accessibility, disclosure, and brand requirements are recorded.

## Research and script
- [ ] Time-sensitive or factual claims have reliable sources.
- [ ] Opinions, estimates, and creative reconstructions are distinguished from facts.
- [ ] Script wording is approved; pronunciation and regional language are checked.
- [ ] Narration sounds natural in the requested locale; robotic or rushed takes are rejected.
- [ ] Estimated speaking time fits the target duration.

## Storyboard and assets
- [ ] Every scene has a purpose, action, framing, camera direction, and timing.
- [ ] Continuity is tracked for people, products, props, locations, and lighting.
- [ ] Each prompt begins from the previous clip's actual end state; narration matches visible action.
- [ ] Reference images do not pre-spoil later recipe states or conflict with the scene action.
- [ ] Every asset has a source, rights basis, and usage scope recorded.
- [ ] Required consent for identifiable faces and voices is documented.
- [ ] Media paths resolve and technical specifications are suitable.

## Edit and audio
- [ ] Timeline FPS and frame boundaries are consistent.
- [ ] Transitions have an editorial purpose and enough clip handles.
- [ ] Narration, captions, music, and effects are synchronized.
- [ ] Dialogue is intelligible; peaks do not clip.
- [ ] Text is readable on the target device and within safe areas.

## Render and QA
- [ ] Export settings match the delivery brief.
- [ ] Output file exists and metadata has been checked.
- [ ] Complete video has been watched at normal speed.
- [ ] Scene boundaries, transitions, first/last frames, and critical actions have been inspected.
- [ ] Complete audio has been listened to on appropriate playback devices.
- [ ] Audio review rejects robotic cadence, wrong regional accent, excessive speed, and masked speech.
- [ ] Captions, spelling, links, credits, and disclosure are checked.

## Handoff
- [ ] Final media and editable source are clearly named.
- [ ] Asset manifest and source/licensing notes are included.
- [ ] Known limitations are disclosed.
- [ ] Status is accurately labeled: generated, structurally checked, visually reviewed, audio reviewed, or approved.
