# End-to-end workflow

## 1. Intake
Identify the subject, objective, audience, platform, aspect ratio, duration, language, tone, CTA, source material, budget, deadline, and available device/tools. Ask only for missing details that materially affect the result.

## 2. Research
For factual/current subjects, gather trustworthy sources, cross-check key claims, note publication dates, and separate facts from interpretation. For purely creative work, research may be skipped or limited to style references.

## 3. Creative brief
Define audience need, central message, value proposition, emotional tone, format, hook, narrative progression, and intended action. Use `templates/creative-brief.md`.

## 4. Script
Write voiceover/dialogue with scene intent and estimated timing. Estimate speech duration realistically; do not cram excessive words into a short clip. Preserve exact user-supplied narration when requested.

## 5. Storyboard
Break the script into timed shots. Specify what is visible, what changes, framing, camera movement, subject action, audio, transition, and continuity state. Use `templates/storyboard.md`.

## 6. Asset production
Create a manifest for each asset: filename/ID, type, scene, dimensions, duration, prompt/source, rights status, and continuity notes. Avoid unnecessary reference images if they create visual mismatch.

## 7. Edit
Assemble scenes on a frame-accurate timeline. Match action to narration, use purposeful transitions, mix voice/music/SFX, and keep typography readable within platform safe areas.

## 8. QA
Run the checks in `modules/quality-assurance.md`. Fix issues and re-render as needed.

## 9. Delivery
Deliver the requested files and a concise handoff. Include source notes and credits for factual or externally sourced content. Do not claim an MP4 exists unless it was actually rendered.
