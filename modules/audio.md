# Audio and sound design module

## Purpose
Make narration, dialogue, music, ambience, and sound effects clear, coherent, and synchronized.

## Workflow
1. Identify whether the format needs narration, dialogue, ASMR, music-led storytelling, or natural sound.
2. Choose a voice and delivery that fit the audience, language, and emotional tone.
3. Obtain consent and rights for voice cloning or synthetic likenesses.
4. Edit narration for intelligibility, pacing, breaths, and clean starts/ends.
5. Add ambience and effects to support visible actions and scene transitions.
6. Mix music beneath speech and preserve headroom to avoid clipping.
7. Check the mix on headphones and a typical phone speaker.

## Synchronization
Align sound effects to the exact visible action. Avoid effects that imply an action not shown. Match scene cuts, audio fades, and music transitions deliberately.

## Rights
Use original, public-domain, or properly licensed music and effects. Verify commercial/social advertising coverage and retain license evidence. Do not reproduce copyrighted songs on request unless the user has supplied authorized material or the work is otherwise permitted.
