# Creative direction and storyboard module

## Purpose
Translate the script and brief into a shootable or generatable visual plan.

## Direction fields
For every scene specify:
- scene ID and time range;
- narration/dialogue or silent beat;
- subject, action, and emotional intention;
- shot size, angle, lens feel, and camera movement;
- composition, lighting, color, environment, and props;
- sound design and transition;
- continuity state at the end of the scene.

## Continuity
Lock recurring character appearance, wardrobe, age presentation, location, prop positions, lighting, camera direction, and visual style. Each following scene must begin from the actual state established by the previous scene.

## Camera language
Choose camera moves intentionally: static for clarity, push-in for emphasis, tracking for movement, pan/tilt for reveals, orbit for spatial context, handheld for immediacy, and overhead for process demonstrations. Avoid movement that distracts or causes generation instability.

## Prompt writing
Describe one coherent shot with explicit subject, action, environment, camera, lighting, style, duration, and constraints. Keep spoken narration in the requested language and specify it explicitly. Do not rely on vague terms such as "cinematic" alone.

## Deliverables
Timed storyboard, shot list, continuity lock, generation prompts, and asset requirements.
