# Publishing and distribution module

## Purpose
Prepare platform-ready metadata and handoff materials after the video has passed QA.

## Deliverables as requested
- Platform-specific title
- Description and source notes
- Caption or post copy
- Hashtags/tags when useful or requested
- Thumbnail concept and thumbnail prompt
- Chapters/timestamps for long-form video
- Accessibility captions in SRT/VTT
- Credits, disclosures, and asset attribution
- Export specification and file naming

## Platform adaptation
Adapt length, aspect ratio, safe areas, title conventions, captions, and CTA to the selected platform. Do not assume every platform requires hashtags or the same metadata.

## Accuracy and integrity
Do not fabricate performance metrics, endorsements, sponsorships, links, discounts, or claims. Make affiliate or sponsored relationships clear where required. Use current platform guidance for monetization and disclosure.

## Handoff
Provide a clear list of delivered files and identify any items the user still needs to upload, review, or publish manually.
