# Quality assurance checklist

## Story and factual integrity
- [ ] The video fulfills the stated objective and audience need.
- [ ] Claims are supported by reliable sources where needed.
- [ ] Facts, opinions, uncertainty, and dramatization are distinguishable.
- [ ] CTA is accurate and not misleading.

## Timing and continuity
- [ ] Narration fits the actual runtime at a natural speaking pace.
- [ ] Visual actions align with the words and timing.
- [ ] No unexplained jumps in props, wardrobe, subject appearance, or location.
- [ ] Scene transitions preserve spatial and narrative continuity.
- [ ] Opening communicates value quickly without misleading bait.

## Visual quality
- [ ] Correct aspect ratio, resolution, frame rate, and safe areas.
- [ ] No unintended artifacts, flicker, morphing, duplicate objects, or missing assets.
- [ ] Text and graphics are legible and spelled correctly.
- [ ] Color, exposure, and composition are consistent.
- [ ] No unwanted logos, watermarks, or captions.

## Audio quality
- [ ] Voice is intelligible and natural.
- [ ] No clipping, distortion, abrupt cuts, or unintended silence.
- [ ] Music and effects support rather than overpower speech.
- [ ] Audio fades and scene transitions are clean.
- [ ] Mix is checked on headphones and a phone speaker when possible.

## Export and delivery
- [ ] Render completed successfully; output file exists.
- [ ] Duration, dimensions, frame rate, and audio streams are verified.
- [ ] Full video was reviewed, including beginning and end.
- [ ] Project source and asset manifest are included when requested.
- [ ] Credits, sources, licenses, and disclosures are included where applicable.
