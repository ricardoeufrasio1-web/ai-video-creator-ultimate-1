# Research and fact-checking module

## Purpose
Build a reliable evidence base for videos that explain real-world subjects, current events, science, history, products, people, or public claims.

## Procedure
1. Identify which claims require verification and which parts are creative opinion.
2. Prefer primary sources: official datasets, papers, legal documents, original interviews, company filings, and direct statements.
3. Use reputable secondary sources to add context and independent verification.
4. Cross-check important claims with more than one independent source when feasible.
5. Record title, publisher, author, publication date, URL, access date, and the exact claim supported.
6. Flag conflicts, missing evidence, uncertainty, and outdated information.
7. Avoid presenting speculation, reconstructed dialogue, or AI-generated scenes as documented fact.

## Output
- Research question and scope
- Verified fact list
- Claim-to-source map
- Important caveats and uncertainty
- Suggested narration-safe wording
- Source notes for credits/description

## Special care
For health, legal, financial, political, and safety topics, use authoritative sources, avoid personalized professional advice, and state limitations clearly.
