# Scriptwriting and retention module

## Purpose
Create clear, engaging scripts for short-form and long-form video without sacrificing accuracy or audience trust.

## Inputs
Audience, objective, platform, duration, language, tone, key message, required facts, CTA, and any supplied script or source material.

## Short-form structure
- **Hook:** immediate visual or verbal reason to keep watching.
- **Promise/context:** establish what the viewer will learn or see.
- **Development:** deliver useful information in logical beats.
- **Payoff:** resolve the opening question or demonstrate the result.
- **CTA:** one clear, relevant next step when appropriate.

## Long-form structure
Use a clear premise, opening stakes, chapter/section progression, examples or evidence, transitions, payoff, and conclusion. Add pattern changes only when they improve comprehension or pacing.

## Timing
Estimate spoken duration from the actual word count and a natural speaking pace for the language and delivery style. Leave room for pauses, reactions, demonstrations, and visual-only moments. Never force an unrealistic amount of speech into a fixed clip length.

## Rules
- Preserve user-provided wording exactly when explicitly requested.
- Do not invent quotes, testimonials, statistics, or guarantees.
- Avoid deceptive hooks and unsupported health, financial, or performance promises.
- Make the CTA match the actual offer and destination.
- Offer alternate hooks only when useful; do not create unnecessary variants.
